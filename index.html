I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Columbia University. My research interests include multimodal learning, large language models, search and recommendation systems, and AI agents.

Previously, I studied Artificial Intelligence and Automation at Huazhong University of Science and Technology and spent an exchange year in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC Santa Barbara.

Research

At HUST's Intelligent Perception and Auto-Driving Lab, I worked on a multimodal LLM agent based on Monkey and Qwen-VL. I built instruction data for tool use, fine-tuned Qwen-VL with LoRA, and integrated external vision tools including Grounding DINO, SAM, and InstructPix2Pix.

Experience

I completed two AI internships at China Telecom, working on financial document intelligence and real-time examination monitoring. My work involved sequence-to-sequence generation, Faster R-CNN, YOLO11, PyTorch, model evaluation, and inference pipelines.
